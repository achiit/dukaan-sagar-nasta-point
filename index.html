Meet Sagar

I started Sagar Nasta Point in 2013 with one idea: serve snacks the way we make them at home. Hot, fresh and with a smile.

Since then, many of our customers have become regulars, and their kids now come with their own pocket money. That is the best compliment we could ask for.

Our motto is simple: Swad Hamara, Muskan Tumhari. We give the taste, you give the smile.
